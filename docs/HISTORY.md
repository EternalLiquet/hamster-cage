# Daily history (#25)

History reads the same repository snapshot and attendance engine as Dashboard. It pages through fourteen policy-local dates at a time. Earlier/newer paging is bounded and can reach the oldest retained source or calendar date. No derived totals are persisted. Raw, manual, correction, policy and calendar updates invalidate the repository snapshot and refresh the list; a visible-only clock keeps the current date/session amount fresh.

Covered and later-unknown dates show recorded credit and, where reliable, required minutes and balance. Unknown coverage is labelled and suppresses a confident balance. HOLIDAY removes the requirement; WFH remains a label and does not remove an expected day. Other exclusions use EXCLUDED. REVIEW, MANUAL and UNKNOWN COVERAGE are textual badges, stacked for large text. Rejected manual facts and orphan corrections retain REVIEW on every supplied boundary/entry date even when the engine cannot construct a session; all conflicting raw/manual copies are considered. Paging includes all retained boundaries and entry dates, including malformed earlier ends. The policy timezone controls midnight and DST boundaries, independently of device travel time.

Issue #81's open History update reuses #79's `reportingCoverage` categories: `COVERED`, `BEFORE_TRACKING` and `UNKNOWN_AFTER_TRACKING`. An empty fresh install shows onboarding instead of a pre-install review list. Earlier dates before a reliable tracking start are collapsed by default; voluntary browsing labels them before tracking and shows no review count, required time or balance. An isolated manual backfill can be a covered island without making intervening earlier dates owed. Gaps after observed tracking began remain visible and actionable as unknown/review.
