# Daily history (#25)

History reads the same repository snapshot and attendance engine as Dashboard. It pages through fourteen policy-local dates at a time. Earlier/newer paging is bounded and can reach the oldest retained source or calendar date. No derived totals are persisted. Raw, manual, correction, policy and calendar updates invalidate the repository snapshot and refresh the list; a visible-only clock keeps the current date/session amount fresh.

Covered and later-unknown dates show recorded credit and, where reliable, required minutes and balance. Unknown coverage is labelled and suppresses a confident balance. HOLIDAY removes the requirement; WFH remains a label and does not remove an expected day. Other exclusions use EXCLUDED. REVIEW, MANUAL and UNKNOWN COVERAGE are textual badges, stacked for large text. Rejected manual facts and orphan corrections retain REVIEW on every supplied boundary/entry date even when the engine cannot construct a session; all conflicting raw/manual copies are considered. Paging includes all retained boundaries and entry dates, including malformed earlier ends. The policy timezone controls midnight and DST boundaries, independently of device travel time.

Issue #81's open History update in draft [PR #92](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/hamster-cage/pull/92) reuses #79's `reportingCoverage` categories: `COVERED`, `BEFORE_TRACKING` and `UNKNOWN_AFTER_TRACKING`. An empty fresh install shows onboarding instead of a pre-install review list. Ordinary dates before a reliable tracking start are collapsed by default; voluntary browsing labels them before tracking and shows no required time or balance. Retained conflicting or rejected raw/manual/correction facts are an exception: their REVIEW findings remain visible by default, count as actionable review and explain the finding without implying required time or balance for that pretracking date. An isolated manual backfill can be a covered island without making intervening earlier dates owed. Gaps after observed tracking began remain visible and actionable as unknown/review. After a prior verifier finding about hidden pretracking REVIEW facts, the local rework passed `HistoryPresentationTest` 15/15, one API 37 `HistoryTest` run 6/6 and required Gradle/security checks. New-head independent verification/review and exact-head CI remain pending.

This slice is a daily ledger. Session explanation and editing belong to #26/#27; no inactive edit controls are shown. Persisted coverage is provided by #19, so until that integration the list truthfully remains provisional. It requires no network, introduces no component/permission and does not log attendance data.

Focused tests cover empty expected days, unknown coverage, holiday/WFH/manual/review semantics, policy-midnight/DST, a thousand synthetic sessions, correction/policy recomputation and fixed page size.
