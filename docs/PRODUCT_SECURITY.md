## Current package boundary

It records bounded current-state facts and unknown gaps, not a continuous route. `WAKE_LOCK` belongs to WorkManager; `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` supports recovery. No foreground location service, analytics or attendance upload. |
| Permissions and IPC | Both variants declare `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`, `INTERNET`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, `WAKE_LOCK` and the package-scoped signature `DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`.
