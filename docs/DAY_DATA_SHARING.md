# Share one day's data

On Dashboard Today or a History date, open the day detail and tap **Share this day's data**. Review the short preview, then either choose an app or save a copy to a file, and send the JSON file only to a trusted recipient. To discuss a device problem privately, attach that JSON file in your private chat. No screenshots or coordinate lookup are needed.

The version-2 JSON covers the selected date in the attendance policy's timezone, from local midnight inclusive to the next local midnight exclusive. These are actual instants, so DST days can be 23 or 25 hours. It also includes raw observations linked to sessions touching that date and at most one earlier observation per related office from the preceding local day. If an EXIT occurs in the final two hours, then for the latest such EXIT it can include the first same-office return and first different-office presence within the first two hours of the next local day. For geofence registration it includes every registration attempt started on the selected date and, separately for every office with retained attempts, that office's latest attempt within the seven days before the date, including offices without callbacks. It includes the cycles those attempts belong to, subsequent cycles through the selected day (including removals and failed synchronization without adds), and the latest preceding cycle within the seven-day lookback even when there are no preceding attempts. Parent cycle times older than the lookback are written as `beforeLookback`, preserving correlation without disclosing those old times. Offices are registered one call at a time, so one office's later registration never hides another's. The preview reports the observation, registration-attempt and cycle counts, with preceding context counted separately, and the evidence date span before a file is made; a registration from earlier days widens that span and is shown in it. Longer-range evidence can still affect derivation, so this bounded file is diagnostic context rather than a guaranteed full engine replay.

The file includes immutable observation type, event time, receipt time, available location-fix time, source, and accuracy; the engine classifications each observation was derived with (for example candidate, rejected or delayed EXIT, receipt-timed or recovery presence), because those depend on stored history beyond the file's bounds; aliases that connect related facts and sessions within this export; relevant manual intervals, corrections without notes, office radius/grace and policy, capture health including the most recent outage and recovery window, app version, preparation time, and the resulting day summary. Capture status describes the device when the file is prepared, not the selected day. Linked late checks can widen the preview's evidence span, but unrelated capture times outside the attendance and registration context are written only as `beforeSpan` or `afterSpan`; the latest check result is included only within that narrower context. Alias numbers are stable within the same export but do not reveal stored IDs. Office names, exact coordinates, notes, credentials, and logs are omitted. Attendance times and patterns are still private. The file is diagnostic evidence, not a backup or an importable archive.

Nothing is sent automatically. Preview cancellation makes no file. Choosing an app creates an app-private temporary JSON file exposed only through a narrow read grant to the selected share target. Android share targets can keep their own copy. The app removes its temporary copy after one hour while running or on a later launch after that time; until then it remains in Android app-private cache. Deleting attendance history also deletes these app-owned temporary copies. If the share sheet is dismissed, the temporary file may remain in that cache until cleanup. Sharing does not change saved attendance, policy, or calculation results. No direct remote read or automatic future-day access is established.

**Save to file** uses the same reviewed preview. Before the system file picker opens, that exact JSON is staged in the same app-private cache. The picker creates a document where you choose (for example Downloads or a cloud drive), the app writes the staged bytes there, and only then reports success. Cancelling, a provider error, or an incomplete write reports that nothing was saved, and the app tries to remove the empty document the picker created. If history is deleted while the picker is open, the staged copy is deleted and nothing is written. No storage permission is requested. A saved copy is outside Hamster Cage: deleting history or uninstalling the app does not remove it, and a location that syncs (such as a cloud drive) uploads it. The app itself sends nothing.

## Registration and location-fix diagnostics (version 2)

These fields are diagnostic only. They never change attendance credit, sessions, confidence or review reasons.

- **`registrationCycles`.** Each cycle records:
  - `kind`: `SYNC` removes the previous set and adds every desired office; `RESYNC` re-adds an office with an ENTER initial trigger after a rejected EXIT; `REMOVE` removes fences because setup, permission or monitoring is not ready.
  - `reason`: the trigger reported by the caller. `STATE_CHANGED` means stored offices or the monitoring setting changed, without claiming which. `UNKNOWN` is used when a caller gives no reason. No reason is inferred.
  - The removal step, whether `cleanup` removed earlier successful adds after a failed or partial cycle, and an outcome.
- **`registrations`.** Each actual `addGeofences` call records its office alias, office edit version, initial-trigger bitmask, start time, completion time, status and error code. The row is saved as `PENDING` before Play services is called.
  - A cancelled call, or one whose result never arrived because the process ended, becomes `INTERRUPTED`. That means the outcome is unknown, not success.
  - A skipped no-op check is not an attempt and is not recorded. A not-ready removal is recorded only when it could remove fences applied in that process, or when the check was forced.
- **Times.** Registration times are wall-clock times comparable with an observation's `receivedAt`. A registration that happens shortly before an observation does not establish that it caused it.
- **Error codes.** Codes are limited to `API_<Play services status>` (for example `API_1000`), `PERMISSION` or `OTHER`. Messages, stack traces and request IDs are never stored.
- **Retention.** Registration diagnostics are pruned when cycles or attempts start, with a 30-day age bound and caps of 2,000 cycles and 5,000 attempts. Pruning removes entire cycles and their attempts together, so retained attempts keep their parent outcome and cleanup evidence. A cycle can be removed earlier than 30 days when a count cap is reached. Deleting attendance history deletes them in the same transaction. A late Play services result only updates an existing `PENDING` row, so it cannot recreate deleted diagnostics. The screen cancels its diagnostics subscription during a privacy reset and waits for fresh attendance, coverage and diagnostics queries under the new reset generation before enabling export.
  - `registrationDiagnostics.oldestRetainedAttemptAt` shows where the retained log begins. No attempts before that point does not mean none happened.
  - `registrationDiagnostics` is `null` when the log could not be read.
- **Location-fix metadata.** Each observation also carries `fixAgeMs`, `provider` and `isMock`:
  - Coverage is geofence deliveries only (`fixMetadataSources`). Other observation sources leave these fields `null`.
  - **`fixAgeMs`** is measured once, when the broadcast arrives and before any queued processing, from the device's elapsed-realtime clock and the fix's own elapsed-realtime timestamp. It is `null` when either is missing or the fix appears newer than receipt.
  - **`provider`** is `fused`, `gps`, `network`, `passive` or `other`.
  - **`isMock`** is the platform's mock flag for the delivered fix, read through `LocationCompat` so it works on older Android versions. `false` means not flagged.
  - Observations recorded before version 2, or delivered without a fix, have `null` in all three fields. A redelivered observation keeps the first-seen values.
  - Accuracy describes uncertainty, not freshness. Use `fixAgeMs` for freshness.

## Boundary check diagnostics (#135)

After an EXIT delivery the app schedules one inexact bounded worker that requests up to five spaced one-shot location checks; a pre-update queue of separate attempts remains readable. These fields explain each actual attempt. They are diagnostic only: a check affects attendance solely through the PRESENCE or ABSENCE observation it records, never through this log.

- **`boundaryChecks`.** One row per attempt whose outcome was recorded, for observations included in this export: the observation's `fact` alias, `attempt` (1–5), `startedAt`, `finishedAt`, `outcome`, and when a fix was obtained its `accuracyMeters` and `fixAgeMs` (wall-clock age when the result was evaluated). Checks for observations outside the export are left out.
- **Outcomes.** `RECORDED_INSIDE` / `RECORDED_OUTSIDE` wrote an observation at its fix time, including a late current-state recovery that does not decide the old EXIT. Skips before a location request: `MONITORING_OFF`, `PREREQUISITES_MISSING`, `REGISTRATION_REFRESH_FAILED`, `REGISTRATION_NOT_ACTIVE` (also an open capture outage), `STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE`, `STARTED_PAST_DEADLINE` (legacy attempt began after the five-minute window), `DEADLINE_PASSED_DURING_PREPARATION`, `NOT_AWAITING_VERIFICATION` and `SUPERSEDED_OR_CHANGED`. After a request: `FIX_UNAVAILABLE`, `CHANGED_DURING_FIX`, `FIX_CACHED` (the fix predates its required observation boundary), `FIX_STALE`, `FIX_NO_ACCURACY`, `FIX_IMPRECISE`, `BOUNDARY_UNCERTAIN`, `FIX_PAST_DEADLINE`, `SETTLED_BEFORE_FIX`, `WRITE_FAILED` and `ERROR`.
- **Missing attempts.** A missing attempt number means that attempt's outcome is unknown (`missingAttemptOutcome` is `UNKNOWN`). It does not show the attempt never ran: it may not have started, may have been cancelled while running (for example when a newer delivery replaced the scheduled checks), may have recorded nothing because a privacy reset began, may have failed to save its row (busy capture gate or a storage error), or may have been pruned.
- **Older rows.** Logs written before the deadline cases were separated store both as one value; they are read back as `PAST_DEADLINE_BEFORE_REQUEST`, meaning the window had closed before any location request without saying why.
- **Storage and retention.** Rows live in app-private storage excluded from backup, keeping the newest 1,000 started within 30 days. Writes share the capture gate with history deletion and are skipped for an older privacy generation, so a late attempt cannot recreate rows a reset removed. Deleting attendance history clears the log. `boundaryCheckDiagnostics` is `null` when the log could not be read, and `oldestRetainedCheckAt` shows where the retained log begins.
- **Preview.** The share preview counts the included recorded check attempts.
