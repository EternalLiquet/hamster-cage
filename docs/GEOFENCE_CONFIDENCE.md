# Office-area boundary confidence (#101)

Android recommends a geofence radius of at least 100–150 meters and notes that larger areas work better for home/work style places. Background geofence delivery can take minutes. New Hamster Cage offices default to **200 m** as a stability-oriented office-area boundary. The allowed 50–5000 m range remains under user control: saved radii are never rewritten. Setup and saved-office cards warn below 150 m and when two enabled attendance office areas touch or overlap. The app never interprets a geofence as a building wall or badge event. [Android guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/geofencing)

Every Play Services ENTER/EXIT remains an immutable local raw fact. When the triggering location includes usable accuracy, the app stores the accuracy in meters with receipt and observed-fix times, but stores no coordinates. Bounded foreground, adaptive and periodic fixes also retain their accuracy with appended presence/absence facts. A fix can classify one office as inside only if its whole accuracy circle lies within that eligible office and does not plausibly touch another eligible office. A fix wholly outside all eligible offices can establish current absence. Boundary overlap, poor or stale accuracy, approximate-only permission and location-off states do not produce a presence/absence fact. Normal history does not display coordinates, accuracy numbers or raw IDs.

After a *new* geofence boundary fact commits, one unique, inexact WorkManager check for that office is requested after 45 seconds. A newer fact replaces that pending check; duplicate delivery does not postpone it. The job requests at most one fresh current-location fix with a 15-second request bound. It rechecks monitoring, permissions, privacy-reset generation, office version, registration and newest evidence both before and after the request. Editing or disabling the office, opting out, or resetting history makes pending work ineffective; opt-out/reset also cancels pending jobs. WorkManager and Android background restrictions may delay or suppress the check. This is an opportunistic short path, not a guaranteed timer, continuous GPS, exact alarm or foreground service. Weekday reconciliation remains the long-stop recovery path.

A fresh, clearly inside adaptive fix after an isolated same-office EXIT within three minutes can corroborate continuity. Derivation retains both raw observations and the original opening/grace anchor; an intervening absence or other-office fact forbids the bridge. A pre-recovery visit spanning a registration outage is split at the fix instead of credited backward. After three minutes, the fix establishes a new visit only at its own observed time, with normal arrival grace and an unknown gap. A clearly outside fix corroborates the current absence at its observed time. While the latest EXIT lacks independent corroboration, one boundary review remains instead of a confident physical-departure claim. Timeout or ambiguous accuracy retains that review and does not invent an observed crossing. A later inside fix can resume credit; there is no claim about unobserved earlier time.
