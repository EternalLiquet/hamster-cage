# Office-area boundary confidence (#101)

Android recommends a geofence radius of at least 100–150 meters and notes that larger areas work better for home/work style places. Background geofence delivery can take minutes. New Hamster Cage offices default to **200 m** as a stability-oriented office-area boundary. The allowed 50–5000 m range remains under user control: saved radii are never rewritten. Setup and saved-office cards warn below 150 m and when two enabled attendance office areas touch or overlap. The app never interprets a geofence as a building wall or badge event. [Android guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/geofencing)

Every Play Services ENTER/EXIT remains an immutable local raw fact. When the triggering location includes usable accuracy, the app stores the accuracy in meters with receipt and observed-fix times, but stores no coordinates. Bounded foreground, adaptive and periodic fixes also retain their accuracy with appended presence/absence facts. An inconclusive bounded fix stores only its last accuracy and sanitized outcome in app-private monitoring diagnostics; it does not manufacture a PRESENCE/ABSENCE fact. A fix can classify one office as inside only if its whole accuracy circle lies within that eligible office and does not plausibly touch another eligible office. A fix wholly outside all eligible offices can establish current absence. Boundary overlap, poor or stale accuracy, approximate-only permission and location-off states do not produce a presence/absence fact. Normal history does not display coordinates, accuracy numbers or raw IDs.

After a *new* geofence boundary delivery commits, the app requests inexact WorkManager work under one global unique name, even if the delivery names several offices. A newer delivery replaces pending work; duplicate delivery does not postpone it. The receiver waits for the enqueue operation while holding its capture gate so older work cannot replace newer work out of order. Each check requests at most one current-location fix with a 15-second request bound and rechecks monitoring, permissions, privacy-reset generation, affected office versions, registration and newest evidence before and after the request. Fixes up to 30 seconds old are accepted only when wall and monotonic ages agree within one second and their observed time follows the boundary and latest recovery; the request start itself is not an observation boundary. Editing or disabling an affected office or resetting history makes pending work ineffective; opting out cancels it. WorkManager and Android background restrictions may delay or stop work. This is an opportunistic short path, not a guaranteed timer, continuous GPS, exact alarm or foreground service.

For an ENTER, one check runs after about 45 seconds. A fresh, clearly inside fix within three minutes of an isolated same-office EXIT can corroborate continuity; an intervening absence or other-office fact forbids the bridge, and a pre-recovery visit spanning a registration outage is split at the fix instead of credited backward.

### Bounded EXIT verification

An EXIT is a candidate departure, not an immediate boundary. One bounded worker starts inexactly around 30 seconds after receipt and, if still running, makes sparse one-shot checks around 90, 150, 210 and 270 seconds. Missed slots are skipped, not replayed in a bunch. Previously queued independent checks remain compatible. A verification fix is accepted only if observed within five minutes of receipt. Inside and outside samples are stored as their own immutable sources and pass the same decisive geometry gate; poor indoor accuracy produces no sample rather than a guess.

- **Pending.** While a check can still be accepted, the visit stays open but credit stops at the EXIT's observed time and the Dashboard shows that the exit is awaiting confirmation.
- **Confirmed departure.** Two decisive outside samples at least 45 seconds apart, or a decisive fix at another office, confirm the departure at the raw EXIT time. Verification latency is never credited.
- **Rejected phantom EXIT.** Inside evidence must be continuous after the latest EXIT: at least four decisive inside samples with distinct observation times, no stretch from the EXIT's own observation to the first sample or between samples longer than three minutes, the last sample at least four minutes after the EXIT, and no outside sample. Four of the five checks suffice, so one ambiguous indoor fix does not by itself prevent a rejection. A repeatedly returned cached fix counts only once. Android delivery and WorkManager delays can still leave the EXIT unresolved; neither delayed nor bunched evidence extends credit.
- **Unresolved.** When the window passes without a decision, the EXIT is no longer shown as awaiting confirmation. It becomes an unresolved departure under `UNCONFIRMED_BOUNDARY` review and the visit ends at the EXIT. Its samples stay evidence only: they neither restore the visit through the 60-second bounce rule nor open a new one. A single overdue eligible check may instead establish current presence at its actual fix time as an ordinary recovery fact; it does not decide the old EXIT or credit the uncertain gap. A later routine check or platform ENTER can do the same.

Each EXIT's verification is bounded by its own window: a later EXIT starts new verification and can never be rejected with samples taken before it.

A late delivery never erases attendance that was observed before the EXIT: the earlier visit keeps its ENTER-to-EXIT credit and only the unobserved interval after the EXIT is uncertain. Only a genuine capture outage, or another office's evidence since the visit opened, makes a later inside fix an unsafe recovery that splits the old visit; its credit runs only through the latest same-office current-location fix that placed the user inside, and only up to a later same-office ENTER that is neither a merged bounce nor an immediate re-entry right after another inside observation (such as the re-arm after a rejected EXIT), or another office's arrival or fix, either of which shows the user may have left (an opening ENTER alone earns nothing); the uncertain remainder is uncredited and reviewable. A visit that opened after the previous recovery and whose EXIT was observed no later than the start of the latest outage lies wholly inside one healthy capture window and is not split. Later samples in the same verification continue the new visit rather than splitting again.

After a rejection the platform may still consider the device outside and report no EXIT for a later real departure. The app therefore re-adds that office's geofence with the same request ID and an initial ENTER trigger, which Android documents as reporting ENTER only if the device is already inside; that ENTER carries its own observation time, corroborates the open visit and creates no earlier arrival. The bounded worker re-arms an unresolved EXIT after its checks finish, outside the capture gate; the pre-update queue does so on its final attempt. A newer observation supersedes the re-arm (#140). The bounded worker begins no earlier than its first delayed slot, and re-arm only acts on a still-undecided EXIT; an early re-arm following a fast terminal failure could otherwise generate a quick-return ENTER, so the re-arm is deferred until at least 270 seconds after receipt. A later ENTER opens a visit at its own time. Re-arm does not run without location prerequisites or with monitoring off, and never cancels a partial re-registration. Whether Play services actually reports ENTER after a re-arm has not been verified on a phone. Until the platform reports again, weekday checks record a corroborating inside observation while that visit stays open. If a later check finds the user outside, or the user is next observed at another office, credit runs only to the last same-office inside observation in the visit; the remaining span is uncredited under review.

### Process recreation versus capture outage

For a reboot while this app process is alive, #150 adds a runtime-only protected shutdown callback. It records a separate one-use marker only after live checks of prerequisites, monitoring, privacy generation, delivery health, a surviving geofence PendingIntent, and the matching boot/package registration. The immediately following boot may place the outage at that shutdown signal instead of hours-old `lastHealthyAt`; absent or mismatched evidence keeps the old conservative boundary. It never extends a visit from a new location observation or infers an EXIT. The callback cannot run after the process was reclaimed, and a surviving PendingIntent retains the Play services limitation below. The marker is discarded after first process recovery or subsequent health changes; real phone behavior is unverified.

Android reclaims background app processes routinely without affecting geofences that Play services holds for the app. Registration is lost after a reboot, an app update, a data clear or a force-stop. Before Android 15 a force-stop does not cancel the app's PendingIntents, so a surviving token alone cannot rule one out (accepted below for an ordinary restart); from Android 15 the system cancels them and also reports `wasForceStopped`. At the first capture work in a new process the app gathers, before re-registering:

- whether the geofence PendingIntent still exists, and whether the boot count and app update time still match those recorded at the last successful registration;
- on Android 11 and later, why the previous process ended, if that record was written after the last confirmed capture health;
- on Android 15 and later, whether this start followed a force-stop;
- whether the work began from a background job (recorded, but not decisive: opening the app after a force-stop lets overdue work run first).

Any sign of loss is an outage: a missing token, a reboot, an update, a force-stop, or a previous exit recorded as a user stop, permission change or package change. A previous exit recorded as user-requested (a Settings force-stop, a task-manager stop, or on some devices removal from recents) is also an outage on Android 11–14, where it may be a force-stop that keeps the token; a background start does not change that, because opening the app after a force-stop lets overdue work run first. Otherwise a present token with an unchanged app update time is treated as continuous monitoring (#142). The token rules out a reboot and, as expected, an app data clear; from Android 15 it also rules out a force-stop, which cancels the app's PendingIntents, so an ordinary reclaim does not cost confirmed presence for the rest of the day. An unreadable token or app update time keeps the outage behaviour: dates since the last confirmed health become unknown, and a new observation is required before presence is confirmed. Accepted risk: Android 8–10 record no exit reason, so a force-stop there is not detectable; on Android 11–14 neither is a force-stop applied while no process was running; and clearing Play services data or updating it may drop geofences while the app's token remains. During such an undetected lapse, a departure and return that the platform also missed would be credited.

A synthetic stationary example illustrates the rules: ENTER 09:00, EXIT 11:00 delivered 30 seconds later, and inside samples at 11:01, 11:02, 11:03 and 11:04 reject the EXIT, so the visit continues from 09:00 with one arrival grace. If the samples are bunched within one minute, or the first one comes after 11:03, the EXIT is unresolved after 11:05:30, credit ends at 11:00 and the samples start nothing; the next routine check establishes current presence. If the visit continues after a rejection and a weekday check at 12:30 finds the user outside, with a corroborating inside check at 12:00, credit runs to 12:00 and 12:00–12:30 is reviewable. Tests cover these sequences plus delayed delivery, bunched and contradictory samples, cross-office movement and process recreation. Physical-phone stationary boundary, genuine departure, poor indoor accuracy, background/screen-off, reboot, force-stop, Wi-Fi variation and OEM battery behaviour remain unverified. No location or attendance is uploaded and no Physical Activity permission is added.
