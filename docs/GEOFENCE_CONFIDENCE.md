# Office-area boundary confidence (#101)

Android recommends a geofence radius of at least 100–150 meters and notes that larger areas work better for home/work style places. Background geofence delivery can take minutes. New Hamster Cage offices default to **200 m** as a stability-oriented office-area boundary. The allowed 50–5000 m range remains under user control: saved radii are never rewritten. Setup and saved-office cards warn below 150 m and when two enabled attendance office areas touch or overlap. The app never interprets a geofence as a building wall or badge event. [Android guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/geofencing)

Every Play Services ENTER/EXIT remains an immutable local raw fact. When the triggering location includes usable accuracy, the app stores the accuracy in meters with receipt and observed-fix times, but stores no coordinates. Bounded foreground, adaptive and periodic fixes also retain their accuracy with appended presence/absence facts. An inconclusive bounded fix stores only its last accuracy and sanitized outcome in app-private monitoring diagnostics; it does not manufacture a PRESENCE/ABSENCE fact. A fix can classify one office as inside only if its whole accuracy circle lies within that eligible office and does not plausibly touch another eligible office. A fix wholly outside all eligible offices can establish current absence. Boundary overlap, poor or stale accuracy, approximate-only permission and location-off states do not produce a presence/absence fact. Normal history does not display coordinates, accuracy numbers or raw IDs.

After a *new* geofence boundary delivery commits, the app requests inexact one-shot WorkManager checks under one global unique name, even if the delivery names several offices. A newer delivery replaces pending checks; duplicate delivery does not postpone them. The receiver waits for the enqueue operation while holding its capture gate so older work cannot replace newer work out of order. Each check requests at most one fresh current-location fix with a 15-second request bound and rechecks monitoring, permissions, privacy-reset generation, affected office versions, registration and newest evidence before and after the request. Editing or disabling an affected office or resetting history makes pending work ineffective; opting out cancels it. WorkManager and Android background restrictions may delay, bunch or suppress checks. This is an opportunistic short path, not a guaranteed timer, continuous GPS, exact alarm or foreground service.

For an ENTER, one check runs after about 45 seconds. A fresh, clearly inside fix within three minutes of an isolated same-office EXIT can corroborate continuity; an intervening absence or other-office fact forbids the bridge, and a pre-recovery visit spanning a registration outage is split at the fix instead of credited backward.

### Bounded EXIT verification

An EXIT is a candidate departure, not an immediate boundary. Up to five checks target about 30, 90, 150, 210 and 270 seconds after the EXIT was received, and a fix is accepted only if it was observed within five minutes of that receipt. Inside and outside samples are stored as their own immutable sources and pass the same fix gate as every other check: fresh, precise, and with the whole accuracy circle inside exactly one eligible office or wholly outside all of them. Poor indoor accuracy therefore produces no sample rather than a guess; the gate is not relaxed to make verification succeed.

- **Pending.** While a check can still be accepted, the visit stays open but credit stops at the EXIT's observed time and the Dashboard shows that the exit is awaiting confirmation.
- **Confirmed departure.** Two decisive outside samples at least 45 seconds apart, or a decisive fix at another office, confirm the departure at the raw EXIT time. Verification latency is never credited.
- **Rejected phantom EXIT.** At least five inside samples spanning at least four minutes, with no outside sample, keep the visit continuous. The first sample must be observed within three minutes of the EXIT's own observation, the same horizon the engine already uses for a single confirming fix. This compares observation times, so both platform delivery latency and late WorkManager execution count against it. Android documents alert latency as usually under two minutes, about two to three minutes under background location limits and up to six minutes for a stationary device, so a late or bunched set of samples cannot reject an EXIT; it never extends credit.
- **Unresolved.** When the window passes without a decision, the EXIT is no longer shown as awaiting confirmation. It becomes an unresolved departure under `UNCONFIRMED_BOUNDARY` review, credit still ends at the EXIT, and any samples count as ordinary observations at their own times, so a later inside sample begins a new visit with normal arrival grace.

A late delivery never erases attendance that was observed before the EXIT: the earlier visit keeps its ENTER-to-EXIT credit and only the unobserved interval after the EXIT is uncertain. Only a genuine capture outage, or another office's evidence since the visit opened, makes a later inside fix an unsafe recovery that splits the old visit without credit.

After a rejection the platform may still consider the device outside and report no EXIT for a later real departure. The app therefore re-adds that office's geofence with the same request ID and an initial ENTER trigger, which Android documents as reporting ENTER only if the device is already inside; that ENTER carries its own observation time, corroborates the open visit and creates no earlier arrival. Until the platform reports again, weekday checks record a corroborating inside observation while that visit stays open. If a later check finds the user outside, credit runs only to the last same-office inside observation in the visit; the span from there to the outside check is uncredited under `UNCONFIRMED_GAP` review. A visit with no inside evidence after its ENTER keeps the existing behaviour and earns nothing for the uncertain span.

### Process recreation versus capture outage

Android reclaims background app processes routinely. A PendingIntent is a token held by the system that survives its app's process, and the geofences registered with it keep delivering. At the first capture work in a new process, the app checks whether that token still exists before re-registering. A present token means registration continued and no outage is recorded. A missing token (force-stop, reboot, app update or data clear, after which Play services has also discarded the geofences), an exit the system reports as a user stop or package update on Android 11 and later, or a token that cannot be inspected keeps the existing outage behaviour: dates since the last confirmed health become unknown and a new observation is required before presence is confirmed.

Weekday reconciliation is the inexact long-stop when adaptive confirmation is delayed or unavailable. Its intended interval is 30 minutes while outside and 15 minutes while a visit is open or an EXIT remains unconfirmed, the shortest WorkManager periodic interval. Android may delay either interval. A fresh, decisive fix outside an office may append an `ABSENCE` at the fix observation time even when its latest geofence `EXIT` already closed the derived session. This resolves the outstanding boundary review without changing the raw `EXIT` or inventing the physical departure time. A fix inside a different eligible office can likewise corroborate outside for the prior office and establish the new office only from that fix time with normal arrival grace. Receipt time remains separately recorded. A later check in the same unchanged state adds no duplicate fact or credit.

A synthetic stationary example illustrates the rules: ENTER 09:00, EXIT 11:00 delivered 30 seconds later, and inside samples at 11:01, 11:02, 11:03, 11:04 and 11:05 reject the EXIT, so the visit continues from 09:00 with one arrival grace. If the same samples are bunched within one minute, or the first one arrives after 11:03, the EXIT is unresolved after 11:05:30, credit ends at 11:00 and the first sample starts a new visit. If the visit continues after a rejection and a weekday check at 12:30 finds the user outside, with a corroborating inside check at 12:00, credit runs to 12:00 and 12:00–12:30 is reviewable. Tests cover these sequences plus delayed delivery, bunched and contradictory samples, cross-office movement and process recreation. Physical-phone stationary boundary, genuine departure, poor indoor accuracy, background/screen-off, reboot, force-stop, Wi-Fi variation and OEM battery behaviour remain unverified. No location or attendance is uploaded and no Physical Activity permission is added.
