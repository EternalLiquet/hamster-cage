# Explain a day (#26)

History opens a read-only day detail with the exact shared-engine daily total, expected-day reason and explicit unknown coverage. It lists day-clipped credited intervals after the uncredited arrival walking grace, same-office observed-gap reconciliation and global overlap union. Reconciliation may credit the observed EXIT-to-next-ENTER gap only when both adjacent visits earn positive credit. A visit shorter than arrival grace blocks reconciliation across it; no reconciled interval fills the next arrival walking window. Credited intervals may therefore remain split. Each interval retains its contributing session IDs. Raw and effective session bounds, the arrival grace end/credit start, separate exit/departure projection grace, confidence and correction audit entries remain visible. An observed visit ending before credit starts has zero credited time; it remains in the raw timeline.

Every raw observation is retained, including duplicate/conflicting copies. A correction that moves a session to another date does not hide its original events: both the original and effective date link to that evidence. Grace crossing midnight also links to its contributing session. Rejected manual/orphan correction evidence remains visible with review explanations. No source facts are changed or stored by this view.

Draft #88 labels an immutable foreground one-shot `PRESENCE` fact in day detail as a **current-location presence observation**, distinct from a delivered Android geofence ENTER. Its timestamp is the observed fix time, not an inferred arrival; no coordinates are shown or stored in the attendance fact. If it splits an old open raw ENTER across uncertain coverage, day detail calls the old end an inferred bound at the next presence check, not an observed EXIT; that old segment stays uncredited and reviewable even when Today's estimate uses the distinct new session. Local focused UI checks cover the label, while independent PR gates and physical behavior remain open.

All timestamps use the policy zone and include an offset to distinguish DST repeats. Totals are displayed in whole minutes using the same presentation as Dashboard; timestamps retain seconds so sub-minute boundaries remain inspectable. Each evidence section pages by ten entries. Plain-text notes are stripped of control/format characters only for display; saved bytes remain unchanged. Exact office coordinates are not part of the explanation.

Focused examples cover split sessions, grace overlap, reconciled gaps, malformed/duplicate events, holiday/WFH requirement, corrections moving across dates, midnight grace and a 23-hour DST day. UI checks cover History navigation, 200% text, unchanged raw evidence after correction and missing-boundary/unknown-coverage wording. Exact execution and independent gates are recorded in the PR; no phone evidence is implied.
