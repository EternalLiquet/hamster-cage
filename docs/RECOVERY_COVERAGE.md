# Capture recovery and coverage (#19)

The app requests fresh geofence registration after a completed boot, its own package update, the first app launch in a process, an office edit, or a return from device/app settings. Only `BOOT_COMPLETED` and `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED` are manifest receiver actions. The exported receiver checks its action, performs bounded `goAsync` work, starts no service or activity, and the manifest/security audit allows exactly those protected system actions and `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`. The [Android broadcast exception list](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/background-tasks/broadcasts/broadcast-exceptions) includes boot completion, and [Google Play services](https://developers.google.com/android/reference/com/google/android/gms/location/GeofencingClient) says geofences are removed on app reset/upgrade/force-quit. A force-stopped app cannot self-start; opening it requests recovery. Restricted apps may have boot delivery deferred until another start, so opening the app remains a recovery path.

App-private DataStore holds a versioned confidence ledger: first observed date, policy timezone, last confirmed health, an unresolved outage instant, unknown dates, reviewed dates, recovery boundary, last observed event time, and sanitized registration status. It is not an attendance-fact store. Registration alone never starts known history: the first valid observed transition supplies the earliest possible start, and that partial day remains unknown. A policy timezone edit discards old date-only confidence and review, then requires a new post-change observation; immutable Room facts remain. First process use treats prior registration as unproven, marks the interval since last confirmed health uncertain, and re-registers the current enabled office set. Permission loss, no enabled offices, failed removal/registration, and invalid delivery cannot confirm coverage. A new outage invalidates any earlier review of its affected dates; repeated reports of the same unresolved outage preserve review of previously affected dates. The ledger applies policy timezone when assigning dates, including after a delivery failure whose timezone could not immediately be read.

Successful registration closes an outage but does not prove current presence. The dashboard can confirm live presence only after a trustworthy current-day observation at or after the recovery boundary. Draft #88 adds a separate, user-visible foreground one-shot check that may supply such an immutable `PRESENCE` observation when a fresh precise fix unambiguously identifies one eligible office; it never backdates an ENTER. If a same-office raw ENTER from before an outage is still open, the new presence fact splits it: the intervening segment has an inferred end, `UNCONFIRMED_GAP` review and no credit, while a new fix-time session can establish current presence with arrival grace. Only that older same-office gap ending exactly at the single active `PRESENCE` start may be ignored for Today's projection; other review blockers still suppress it, and the old gap remains uncredited/reviewable. Earlier stored ENTER/EXIT facts remain source facts, but cannot revive stale live state or erase that review. A failed, cancelled or inconclusive one-shot check does not repair an outage or make coverage complete. Unknown dates enter the existing domain coverage input; history and balance remain explicitly incomplete until resolved. The app offers a history action for dates needing review and explains the force-stop limitation in location setup. After deliberate history deletion, its generation gate rejects old callbacks, retires old PendingIntents with a bounded durable cursor and records callback timeout as delivery failure; fresh Room facts and coverage are presented together before any confident state returns.

Deterministic tests cover process gap/recovery, timezone assignment, repeated versus new outage review invalidation, empty registration, and post-boundary observation. The manifest audit rejects substituted recovery actions. On a Pixel 10 Pro XL API 37 emulator, two focused Android tests passed for persisted coverage and the visible history-review notice. A host force-stop/relaunch changed the app PID from 3324 to 3752. After a real reboot, the installed receiver was present with boot permission granted, and the app-private coverage file was rewritten after boot; an attempted shell-injected `BOOT_COMPLETED` was rejected by Android as a protected broadcast, so it was not counted as a delivery test. An actual same-package update also rewrote coverage metadata. With a synthetic office and no raw events, host checks showed ACTIVE after permission restoration, NO_OFFICES after disable, ACTIVE after re-enable, non-ACTIVE after background permission revocation, and ACTIVE after restoration. The preview and test packages were then uninstalled. These checks establish registration recovery on this emulator, not background transition delivery or physical-phone battery behavior.

The later [API37 actual Play Services attempt](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/hamster-cage/issues/19#issuecomment-5809761224) registered two synthetic offices and moved emulator GPS while HOME was top, but no raw fact arrived. Provider location remained stale and its GPS request was OFF. This is inconclusive for transition delivery and post-recovery coverage; it cannot substitute for UI-closed or physical-phone evidence. Permissions were restored to denied after the probe.
