# Dashboard contract (#22)

The dashboard derives every total/denominator/balance and departure projection from the same `AttendanceEngine` input/result. It separates device-observed time from policy credit, distinguishes requirements through today from the full-week projection, and shows the five named departure targets. Credited minutes round down for display; a deficit and estimated departure minute round up. A departure on a later local date includes that date.

Issue #78 clarifies the active-arrival presentation: while the office's uncredited arrival walking grace remains, show the time until credit starts and the credit-start timestamp alongside observed in-zone time and credited attendance. Five minutes of grace after three minutes observed means zero credited minutes. Never label arrival grace as earned credit or a bonus. Exit/departure grace belongs only to the projected departure, with coverage uncertainty still visible. The earlier #22 evidence below predates this policy correction and does not verify #78.

Issue #80 restored the Today departure row as the primary guidance: with a valid active session and configured daily target, show a human-readable “You can leave at …” projected geofence-exit time and name the target, even on a fresh install when week and rolling balances are Unknown. The estimate uses only policy-local today credit and the open session, including #78's uncredited arrival delay; it does not assume missing prior days were absences. A dated prior-day orphan correction or invalid observation does not block a clean Today estimate; a current-day or undatable anomaly remains a review blocker. Exit grace changes the projection only, not earned credit. An already-met target says “You can leave now.” Keep a concise provisional/coverage caveat without hiding a bounded time. For an unavailable daily estimate, state the specific reason and next action: distinguish no session or target, future device clock, stale or conflicting boundaries, unresolved corrections/manual intervals, unknown office and an unreachable day/session horizon. “History incomplete” is not a Today explanation. The [focused PR #87](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/hamster-cage/pull/87) passed independent and running-app gates; physical geofence delivery remains a separate gate.

Issue #79 addresses a separate rolling-card presentation gap. On a fresh install, 30/90-day cards must not display the full pre-install requirement or expected-workday count as time owed. With no reliable day, explain that earlier days predate tracking and that balance is unavailable. When a reliable tracked or corrected day exists, displayed credit, expected workdays and requirement must all use the **same covered policy-local dates**; excluded dates add no requirement. Recorded credit on dates without reliable coverage is labelled separately as provisional and does not inflate covered-day progress. A manual-only or orphan-EXIT backfill without a raw ENTER covers only its own reliable day; intervening pre-ledger dates remain pretracking. A retained raw ENTER establishes the observed tracking start even if a later correction moves the effective session earlier or labels it MANUAL: subsequent uncovered dates are later Unknown gaps, not pretracking. Label pretracking dates and later gaps separately, including the affected date/range; mixed coverage keeps balance/average Unknown. The full policy window and immutable attendance facts remain intact. [PR #90](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/hamster-cage/pull/90) passed renewed independent gates and merged. Issue #81 applies the same `reportingCoverage` categories in History: ordinary pretracking dates are collapsed for voluntary browsing, retained REVIEW findings remain actionable even before the boundary, and later unknown gaps remain actionable; its focused independent gates are pending.

Capture readiness is an explicit input, false by default. A registered zone alone never establishes outside state. A clean current-day EXIT and healthy capture are needed to show outside; missing observations stay unknown. Tracking loss takes precedence over an old open session, with review warnings retained. Manual bounds are labelled. Missing history makes balance/average unknown and credit explicitly provisional; a zero is never presented as a confirmed absence without coverage. Exact coordinates are not displayed.

No-office/setup and review notices navigate to Offices or History. Shared dark components, readable status labels and large-text layouts are retained. Storage loading/failure states remain separate and sanitized. The Activity supplies persisted facts; there is no saved running counter. A lifecycle-bound clock refreshes immediately when Dashboard becomes visible and then on minute boundaries, stopping while the activity/page is not visible. Reopening reconstructs duration from stored instants and current time.

The scoped tests cover presentation state and rounding, empty/active/outside/ambiguous/holiday/satisfied/deficit UI, distinct raw/credited and weekly totals, 200% text, and stopped/resumed clock behavior. `scripts/verify_dashboard_restart.py` is an opt-in host check for an actual app force-stop/restart on an emulator. Its test-only fixture seeds only synthetic source data into an empty database, or reuses exactly its own recent synthetic fixture without changing it; unrelated/nondefault source state is refused; it is skipped during ordinary instrumentation and is not counted as an acceptance test. The host check verifies a different process, increased persisted-source credit and continued unknown capture state. It leaves the synthetic fixture for inspection and never resets existing data. Build debug/test APKs before running it; supply `--adb` if platform-tools is not on PATH.
