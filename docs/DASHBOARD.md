# Dashboard contract (#22)

The dashboard derives every total/denominator/balance and departure projection from the same `AttendanceEngine` input/result. It separates device-observed time from policy credit, distinguishes requirements through today from the full-week projection, and shows the five named departure targets. Credited minutes round down for display; a deficit and estimated departure minute round up. A departure on a later local date includes that date.

Issue #78 clarifies the active-arrival presentation: while the office's uncredited arrival walking grace remains, show the time until credit starts and the credit-start timestamp alongside observed in-zone time and credited attendance. Five minutes of grace after three minutes observed means zero credited minutes. Never label arrival grace as earned credit or a bonus. Exit/departure grace belongs only to the projected departure, with coverage uncertainty still visible. The earlier #22 evidence below predates this policy correction and does not verify #78.

Issue #80 restored the Today departure row as the primary guidance: with a valid active session and configured daily target, show a human-readable “You can leave at …” projected geofence-exit time and name the target, even on a fresh install when week and rolling balances are Unknown. The estimate uses only policy-local today credit and the open session, including #78's uncredited arrival delay; it does not assume missing prior days were absences. A dated prior-day orphan correction or invalid observation does not block a clean Today estimate; a current-day or undatable anomaly remains a review blocker. Exit grace changes the projection only, not earned credit. An already-met target says “Goal met. You can leave now.” Keep a concise provisional/coverage caveat without hiding a bounded time. For an unavailable daily estimate, state the specific reason and next action: distinguish no session or target, future device clock, stale or conflicting boundaries, unresolved corrections/manual intervals, unknown office and an unreachable day/session horizon. “History incomplete” is not a Today explanation. The [focused PR #87](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/hamster-cage/pull/87) passed independent and running-app gates; physical geofence delivery remains a separate gate.

Issue #79 addresses a separate rolling-card presentation gap. On a fresh install, 30/90-day cards must not display the full pre-install requirement or expected-workday count as time owed. With no reliable day, explain that earlier days predate tracking and that balance is unavailable. When a reliable tracked or corrected day exists, displayed credit, expected workdays and requirement must all use the **same covered policy-local dates**; excluded dates add no requirement. Recorded credit on dates without reliable coverage is labelled separately as provisional and does not inflate covered-day progress. A manual-only or orphan-EXIT backfill without a raw ENTER covers only its own reliable day; intervening pre-ledger dates remain pretracking. A retained raw ENTER establishes the observed tracking start even if a later correction moves the effective session earlier or labels it MANUAL: subsequent uncovered dates are later Unknown gaps, not pretracking. Label pretracking dates and later gaps separately, including the affected date/range; mixed coverage keeps balance/average Unknown. The full policy window and immutable attendance facts remain intact. A prior [blocking review](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/hamster-cage/pull/90#issuecomment-5822593623) found unmatched full-window credit and missing persisted restart evidence. The repaired #79 head passed independent exact-head verification/review and hosted gates before merge; later physical capture remains separately open.

Capture readiness is an explicit input, false by default. A registered zone alone never establishes outside state. A clean current-day EXIT and healthy capture are needed to show outside; missing observations stay unknown. Tracking loss takes precedence over an old open session, with review warnings retained. Manual bounds are labelled. Missing history makes balance/average unknown and credit explicitly provisional; a zero is never presented as a confirmed absence without coverage. Exact coordinates are not displayed.

No-office/setup and review notices navigate to Offices or History. Shared dark components, readable status labels and large-text layouts are retained. Storage loading/failure states remain separate and sanitized. The Activity supplies persisted facts; there is no saved running counter. A lifecycle-bound clock refreshes immediately when Dashboard becomes visible and then on minute boundaries, stopping while the activity/page is not visible. Reopening reconstructs duration from stored instants and current time.

Issue #118 rewrote the Today panel in everyday words. The default view answers how much counts (“Counted toward today's goal”), how much is still needed and what to do next; arrival delay reads “Time starts counting at …”. Detection, phone-delay and exit-allowance explanations sit behind “How is this worked out?”. Uncertainty stays beside the affected number: incomplete tracking or unconfirmed detection labels the credit “Estimate · …”, remaining time becomes “Unknown”, “About …” or “Maybe none (goal may be met)”, and the leave line says “You can leave at …” only when detection is confirmed, today is fully tracked and no departure is awaiting confirmation. Otherwise it reads “Estimated leave time: about …” or “Goal may be met. Check today's timeline before you leave.” Unconfirmed detection still shows no leave time.
